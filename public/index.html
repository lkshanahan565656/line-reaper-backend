+EV SCREENER — every sportsbook and DFS price scored against the no-vig sharp line (Pinnacle, Circa, exchanges). Positive EV means the price beats the fair odds.
Fair price = vig removed from each sharp book, then averaged. "Consensus" rows had no sharp book, so the fair price is the median of all books: weaker evidence. Kelly shown at quarter stake.

🐋 SHARP TAIL — Polymarket bets settle on-chain, so every wallet has a public record. The backend grades wallets on hundreds of resolved bets (sample, ROI, a luck test, no single lucky longshot, no market making) and turns each new bet by an A (elite) or B (sharp) wallet into a sized signal.
Units are the same for everyone: 1u = 1% of a bankroll, quarter Kelly at the price you can get now at the venue shown, capped at 2u (A) / 1u (B), ×1.5 when 2+ graded wallets agree (max 3u for A, 1.5u for B). Each signal names where to tail it (Kalshi or a US sportsbook, whichever gives the most units after fees) and the most worth paying. A wallet buying more of a bet it already made only tops you up to that size. Kalshi trades are anonymous, so Kalshi shows up as whale flow only.
