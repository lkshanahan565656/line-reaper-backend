BIG MONEY — When a big order hits a bet on Kalshi or Polymarket, the price it paid is what real money thinks the bet is worth. This finds the same bet at the pick'em apps, FanDuel, BetRivers and the US exchanges, and shows where it's cheaper.
Market makers pay to rebalance, not because they know something, so money that worked both sides of a market, hedged on the player's other lines, or came from a market-maker wallet is labelled and hidden unless you show it. Same plays for everyone; every play is logged and graded, and none of this is proven yet.
GAS LOCKS RESEARCH, NOT A BET Kalshi's daily state gas markets settle on the AAA average, which barely moves overnight, yet the favourite is still offered at 90-98¢ the evening before. This tab logs that favourite at its first price and grades it, to test whether the pattern holds. It isn't proven under the 50-graded rule, so no units or profit are shown and nothing here is a bet to place.
Not risk-free: a refinery outage can push a state's price past the line overnight, and one loss at 97¢ costs about 32 wins. Same list for everyone. No alerts.
ARBS — sets of bets at different venues that pay back more than they cost whichever way the game ends, fees included. EXCHANGES checks Kalshi and Polymarket US against each other, and its sportsbook sections add FanDuel and BetRivers. PRICES shows each game line's price on both exchanges side by side. SPORTSBOOKS is the stake splitter for any set of prices. A set is only placeable if every bet is at a venue open where you bet (CUSTOMIZE > My state). Sets with a bet at a closed venue stay listed as reference and sort last. Prices move within minutes: check each one is still there before you place the next.
ARB LAB — scans sportsbooks, sweeps books, and the regulated exchanges (Kalshi · Polymarket · Novig · ProphetX · BetOpenly) for two-sided prices that guarantee profit. Enter a total stake on any hit and it splits it for equal profit either way.
TX MODE keeps only arbs where both legs are playable from Texas / non-betting states. Exchange commissions (ProphetX ≈3% of net wins, Kalshi per-contract fees) are not in the margin — pad thin edges accordingly.

SHARP TAIL follows the Polymarket wallets with the best records and tells you the moment one bets: what, how much, and where you can get it (Kalshi or a US sportsbook).
How it worksPolymarket bets settle on-chain, so every wallet has a public record. The backend grades wallets on hundreds of resolved bets (sample, ROI, a luck test, no single lucky longshot, no market making) and turns each new bet by an A (elite) or B (sharp) wallet into a sized signal.
Units are the same for everyone: 1u = 1% of a bankroll, quarter Kelly at the price you can get now at the venue shown, capped at 2u (A) / 1u (B), ×1.5 when 2+ graded wallets agree (max 3u for A, 1.5u for B). Each signal names where to tail it (Kalshi or a US sportsbook, whichever gives the most units after fees) and the most worth paying. A wallet buying more of a bet it already made only tops you up to that size. Kalshi trades are anonymous, so Kalshi shows up as whale flow only.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Lines come from Underdog and PrizePicks (CS2, Valorant, LoL, Dota). Match plays come from Kalshi and Polymarket.
2. Fair is the books' consensus: Underdog's and Sleeper's payouts and PrizePicks' line each say where the player's middle sits. Our projection (recent maps from bo3.gg, vlr.gg, gol.gg and OpenDota, scaled by the match price) moves it only by the weight its graded record has earned.
3. Plays lists only the +EV sides, grouped by where you'd bet them. All lines shows everything.
4. Tap + to build a slip priced on every book at once, with quarter-Kelly sizing from your bankroll. Model entry lets you enter your own number.
