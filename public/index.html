SHARP TAIL follows the Polymarket wallets with the best records and tells you the moment one bets: what, how much, and where you can get it (Kalshi or a US sportsbook).
How it worksPolymarket bets settle on-chain, so every wallet has a public record. The backend grades wallets on hundreds of resolved bets (sample, ROI, a luck test, no single lucky longshot, no market making) and turns each new bet by an A (elite) or B (sharp) wallet into a sized signal.
Units are the same for everyone: 1u = 1% of a bankroll, quarter Kelly at the price you can get now at the venue shown, capped at 2u (A) / 1u (B), ×1.5 when 2+ graded wallets agree (max 3u for A, 1.5u for B). Each signal names where to tail it (Kalshi or a US sportsbook, whichever gives the most units after fees) and the most worth paying. A wallet buying more of a bet it already made only tops you up to that size. Kalshi trades are anonymous, so Kalshi shows up as whale flow only.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Lines come from Underdog and PrizePicks (CS2, Valorant, LoL, Dota). Match plays come from Kalshi and Polymarket.
2. Fair is the books' consensus: Underdog's and Sleeper's payouts and PrizePicks' line each say where the player's middle sits. Our projection (recent maps from bo3.gg, vlr.gg, gol.gg and OpenDota, scaled by the match price) moves it only by the weight its graded record has earned.
3. Plays lists only the +EV sides, grouped by where you'd bet them. All lines shows everything.
4. Tap + to build a slip priced on every book at once, with quarter-Kelly sizing from your bankroll. Model entry lets you enter your own number.
