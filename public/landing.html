05 / FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is a betting exchange?

Kalshi and Polymarket let you buy Yes or No on an outcome at a price between 1 and 99 cents. If you're right, each contract pays $1. Prices are set by other traders rather than a sportsbook, so they can be slow to react, and that lag is the edge Line Reaper looks for.

What is closing line value, and why does it matter?

The closing line is the last price before a game starts, when the market knows the most. If you bought at 52 cents and it closed at 56, you beat the close by about 8%. Bettors who beat the close consistently win over time, and it shows up after dozens of bets instead of hundreds, which is why every Line Reaper alert is graded on it.

What does "graded two minutes later" mean?

A price at the moment an alert fires only helps if you can still buy it. On exchanges prices often move back before you can act, so we also record the price two minutes after each alert and grade that against the close. A signal only alerts once its own two-minute record is positive. Where a play has not been measured that way yet, the record shows a dash instead of a number.

Do I need to be a professional to use this?

No. Every play tells you where to bet, the price to take and how many units to stake. A unit is just the amount you choose to risk on a standard bet; most people set it at 1% of their bankroll. The Tools tab explains the math behind each number if you want it.

Does Line Reaper place bets for me?

No. We never hold your money, connect to your betting accounts or place bets. You read the alert and decide whether to place it yourself.

Is the advice personalised?

No. Everyone gets the same alert with the same suggested size in units. You choose what a unit is worth to you, and the app can track your own stakes, but it never tailors a bet to your situation.

Will I win?

Not every bet, and nobody can promise you a profit. Even a real edge loses often in the short run, and some of our tools are still losing while their samples grow. What we can promise is the record: every alert is graded in public, including the losers, and plays that stop working are cut.

Where can I use it?

Anywhere the app loads, but you must be 21 or older and follow the laws where you live. Alerts point US users only to US-regulated venues. Exchange and pick'em availability varies by state.

What do I need to get started?

An account on at least one exchange such as Kalshi, and a bankroll you can afford to lose. For esports props, an account on a pick'em app. The free plan needs no card.

Can I get alerts on my phone?

Yes. Open the app on your phone, add it to your home screen, and turn on alerts. Pro plays then arrive as push notifications.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account. Details are in our refund policy.

Who builds it?

A bettor who bets the exchanges every day and built this to do it better. The record at the top of this page is our own, tracked on Pikkit.
