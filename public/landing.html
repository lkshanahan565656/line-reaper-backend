05 / FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is a betting exchange?

Kalshi and Polymarket let you buy Yes or No on an outcome at a price between 1 and 99 cents. If you're right, each contract pays $1. Prices are set by other traders rather than a sportsbook, so they can lag the sharp books. The exchange tools look for that lag; whether it pays is what the record grades.

What is closing line value, and why does it matter?

The closing line is the last price before a game starts, when the market knows the most. If you bought at 52 cents and it closed at 56, you beat the close by about 8%. Bettors who beat the close consistently tend to win over time, and it shows up after dozens of bets instead of hundreds, which is why every Line Reaper alert is graded on it.

What does "graded two minutes later" mean?

A price at the moment an alert fires only helps if you can still buy it. On exchanges prices often move back before you can act, so we also record the price two minutes after each flag and grade that against the close. A proven book sends +EV alerts only while its two-minute grade stays ahead too. Where a play has no two-minute grade yet, the record says "not graded yet" instead of a number.

Do I need to be a professional to use this?

No. Every row shows the venue, the price and the fair it is measured against, and its tag says whether the record proves it. Where a play shows a size it is in units, the same for everyone: a unit is the amount you choose to risk on a standard bet. The Tools tab and Learn explain the math behind each number.

Does Line Reaper place bets for me?

No. We never hold your money, connect to your betting accounts or place bets. You read the alert and decide whether to place it yourself.

Is the advice personalised?

No. Everyone gets the same alert with the same suggested size in units. You choose what a unit is worth to you, and the app can track your own stakes, but it never tailors a bet to your situation.

Will I win?

Not every bet, and nobody can promise you a profit. Even a real edge loses often in the short run, and some of our tools are still losing while their samples grow. What we can promise is the record: every alert is graded in public, including the losers, and plays that stop working are cut.

Where can I use it?

Anywhere the app loads, but you must be 21 or older and follow the laws where you live. Alerts point US users only to US-regulated venues, never to Polymarket's international site. Availability varies by state: in Texas no sportsbook is legal, so a Texan can use the pick'em apps and the CFTC exchanges (Kalshi, Polymarket US, Gemini). What do I need to get started?

An account at a venue that is legal where you live, and a bankroll you can afford to lose. In Texas that is a pick'em app (PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, DK Pick6) or a CFTC exchange (Kalshi, Polymarket US, Gemini). The free plan needs no card.

Can I get alerts on my phone?

Yes. Open the app on your phone, add it to your home screen, then CUSTOMIZE, Phone app, Push alerts. You choose which kinds of alert you get; everyone gets the same text for each.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account. Details are in our refund policy.

Who builds it?

A bettor who bets the exchanges every day and built this to do it better. Every number on this page reads live from the app's own tracker.
