05 / FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is a betting exchange?

Kalshi and Polymarket let you buy Yes or No on an outcome at a price between 1 and 99 cents. If you're right, each contract pays $1. Prices are set by other traders rather than a sportsbook, so the people with an edge leave a trail, and that trail is what Sharp Tail follows.

Do I need to be a professional to use this?

No. Every play tells you where to bet, the price to take and how many units to stake. A unit is just the amount you choose to risk on a standard bet; most people set it at 1% of their bankroll. The Tools tab explains the math behind each number if you want it.

Does Line Reaper place bets for me?

No. We never hold your money, connect to your betting accounts or place bets. You read the alert and decide whether to place it yourself.

Is the advice personalised?

No. Everyone gets the same alert with the same suggested size in units. You choose what a unit is worth to you, and the app can track your own stakes, but it never tailors a bet to your situation.

Will I win?

Not every bet, and nobody can promise you a profit. Even a real edge loses often in the short run. What we can promise is the record: every alert is graded in public, including the losers, so you can judge each tool on its own results.

Where can I use it?

Anywhere the app loads, but you must be 21 or older and follow the laws where you live. Sharp Tail points US users only to US-regulated venues. Exchange and sportsbook availability varies by state.

What do I need to get started?

An account on at least one exchange such as Kalshi, and a bankroll you can afford to lose. For esports props, an account on a supported pick'em app. The free plan needs no card.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account. Details are in our refund policy.

Who builds it?

A bettor who bets the exchanges every day and built this to do it better. The record at the top of this page is our own, tracked on Pikkit.
