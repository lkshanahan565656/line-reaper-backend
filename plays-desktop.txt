Each pick is logged the first time the board shows it +EV and graded at that line and side. ROI treats every pick as a single 1-unit leg at the book's standard payout (PrizePicks ≈ 1.78x). Closing-line value: each pick's ticket (same side, same line, same book) is priced on the books' consensus when it is signaled and again at lock. "Close our way" is the share of picks whose chance moved half a point or more that moved in our favour, and "EV at the close" is what the ticket was worth on the closing market. Over hundreds of picks, beating the close is the surest sign of an edge, long before wins and losses settle it. Picks logged before this was measured show the line move only. Storage: file.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Lines come from Underdog and PrizePicks (CS2, Valorant, LoL, Dota). Match plays come from Kalshi and Polymarket.
2. Fair is the books' consensus: Underdog's and Sleeper's payouts and PrizePicks' line each say where the player's middle sits. Our projection (recent maps from bo3.gg, vlr.gg, gol.gg and OpenDota, scaled by the match price) moves it only by the weight its graded record has earned.
3. Plays lists only the +EV sides, grouped by where you'd bet them. All lines shows everything.
4. Tap + to build a slip priced on every book at once, with quarter-Kelly sizing from your bankroll. Model entry lets you enter your own number.
