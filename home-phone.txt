02 / HOW IT WORKS
From price to bet in three steps.
01
We read the markets

The app reads the exchanges, the pick'em apps, the books we can reach and Pinnacle around the clock, and prices each line against Pinnacle's no-vig fair.

02
The record grades it

Every flag is saved before the game and graded against the close after it. Only a kind that clears the test is called proven, and +EV alerts come only from a proven book.

03
You decide and place it

You place the bet yourself on your own account. The app never places a bet, never holds money and never tailors a bet to you.
