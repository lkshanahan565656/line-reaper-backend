1. Who we are

Line Reaper ("Line Reaper", "we", "us") runs the Line Reaper website and app. Contact us about privacy at the contact address on our legal page.

We do not collect your card details (Stripe handles payment), your sportsbook or exchange logins, your bets on other platforms, your location beyond what an IP address shows, or any government ID.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, analytics pixels or session-recording tools. The app uses browser storage only for the settings above. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. Because we do no cross-site tracking, there is nothing to opt out of; we treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a request not to sell or share, which we never do anyway.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who process it for us under contract, and only as needed:

  • Stripe, for payments and subscription management (Stripe's privacy policy);
  • Railway, which hosts our servers and database in the United States;
  • Google Fonts, as described above; and
  • an email delivery provider, if and when we send account emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or anyone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (in which case this policy keeps applying to your information).

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Public market data we display

Parts of Line Reaper, such as Sharp Tail, analyse public trading activity on prediction markets, including public blockchain wallet addresses and the pseudonymous display names that appear with them. We show this information as it appears publicly, and our grades and labels (such as "sharp") are opinions produced by our models, not statements of fact about any person. If you control a wallet or name we show and want its address and name taken out of what Line Reaper shows, email the contact address on our legal page from an address that shows it is yours, or sign a message with the wallet, and we will remove them within 30 days.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information: until you delete your account.
  • Billing records: Stripe keeps payment records as financial and tax law requires.
  • Server logs: kept by our host for a limited period, usually days to a few weeks.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in backups for up to 35 days before it is overwritten.

7. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live in the US, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it; or
  • delete it. You can delete your account yourself in the app (log in and choose Delete account at the bottom of the app), or email us.

Email the contact address on our legal page from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it is you. We answer within 45 days, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws have these rights by law; we give them to everyone. You can use an authorized agent if your state allows it. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. The Service runs over HTTPS. Payments are handled by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS certified. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you do not use anywhere else. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Age

The Service is only for people aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Where your information is kept

The Service is run from the United States, and your information is stored and processed there.

11. Emails

We send account and billing emails that you need to use the Service. We will only send marketing emails if you agree, and every marketing email will have a one-click unsubscribe.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
