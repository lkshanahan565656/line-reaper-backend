The math behind sharp betting

Short guides to every number Line Reaper shows: fair prices, EV, CLV, hold, arbs, middles, staking, exchange fees and pick’em break-evens. Each example is worked with the same code the app runs.

  • No-vig fair oddsA book’s two prices add up to more than 100%. Taking that margin out gives the fair chance of each side, the number every EV figure is measured against.
  • Expected value (EV)EV is what a bet returns on average at the fair chance. A positive EV price pays more than the outcome is worth.
  • Closing line value (CLV)CLV compares the price you got with the fair price when the market closed. Beating the close over many bets is the most reliable sign that a way of betting has an edge.
  • Hold and arbitrageHold is the margin left at the best price on every side. When the best prices across venues add up to under 100%, the hold is negative: an arb that pays the same whoever wins.
  • MiddlesA middle is Over a low number at one venue and Under a higher one at another. If the result lands between them, both bets win.
  • Kelly stakingKelly sizes a bet by its edge: the bigger the edge and the shorter the odds, the more of the bankroll it is worth. Most bettors use a fraction of it.
  • Exchange fees and effective oddsKalshi, Polymarket US and Gemini sell contracts that pay $1. A price of 55¢ is a 55% chance before fees; the fee turns it into the odds you actually get.
  • Pick’em break-evensPrizePicks, Underdog, DK Pick6 and Betr pay a fixed multiple for a slip. The multiple sets the chance each pick must hit for the slip to break even.
  • How the record worksEvery flag the app makes is logged before the game and graded after it. A kind of bet is only called proven when the record clears a fixed test.
  • GlossaryEvery label and badge in the app, in one or two plain sentences.

Glossary

Every label and badge in the app, in plain words. In the app, tap the ? beside a label, or press and hold it on a phone, for the same definition.

The 50 bar TOWARD 50 · 50 BAR
A kind of bet is judged only once it has 50 graded bets of its own at one venue. Under 50, a record is noise, not a finding. How the record works →
Above fair BEST PRICES ABOVE FAIR · BEST PRICE
A price that pays more than the fair chance is worth, so its EV is positive. It is a price, not a record: check RESULTS for what has held up against the close. Expected value (EV) →
AFTER FEES FEES · FEE
A view (CUSTOMIZE > After fees) that shows each price as the chance you must win to break even after the venue's cut. It changes what is shown, never a stake or an alert. Exchange fees and effective odds →
Alt line
A number other than the venue's main line. Alt lines are graded as their own kind of bet and never counted with main lines.
Arb ARBS
Prices on every side that together cost less than the payout, so the stakes can be split to come out ahead whatever happens. They close fast and a stale or copied price breaks them, so check each app first. Hold and arbitrage →
Ask KALSHI ASK · COST · KALSHI COST · COST AT $50
The lowest price someone will sell a contract for right now. Cost is the ask with the taker fee added.
Beat the close BEAT CLOSE · BEAT IT · LEGS BEAT CLOSE
The share of bets whose price was better than the closing fair. Around half is what chance alone gives.
BEST
The best price you can bet on that side among the venues shown, after fees. Green when it is also above the fair.
Break-even BE · SIDE VS BREAK-EVEN
The hit rate a bet needs to come out even after the venue's cut: an exchange's all-in cost, a book's implied chance, or a pick'em leg's share of its slip's payout. Pick’em break-evens →
Close PIN CLOSE · AT CLOSE · APP CLOSE
The last price before the event starts. Pinnacle's no-vig close is the yardstick every record here is graded against. Closing line value (CLV) →
CLV LEG CLV · SLIP CLV · WEEK CLV · ALL-TIME CLV
Closing line value: the price you got against the fair when the market closed. Beating the close over many bets is the most reliable sign that a way of betting has an edge. Closing line value (CLV) →
COPY (c)
A book's price read second-hand through Action Network. It can be minutes old and is never a play on its own.
DEVIG
The way the margin is taken out of a price: power (the default), multiplicative, additive or Shin. Switching it changes every fair and EV on the page, usually by a fraction of a point. No-vig fair odds →
Edge EDGE IF FILLED · MIN EDGE PTS · PINNACLE − COST · MODEL − COST
The gap, in points of chance, between the fair (or our model) and what the bet costs you. A positive edge is a price the math likes, not a result.
EV EV AT YOURS · MIN EV · MIN EV % · EV TOP
Expected value: what a bet returns on average at the fair chance, as a share of the stake. It is only as right as the fair it is measured against. Expected value (EV) →
Fair PINNACLE FAIR · FAIR AT POST · FAIR AT FLAG · FAIR FIRST
The chance a side really has, read from Pinnacle's price with its margin taken out (Matchbook blended in when it prices the same line). Every EV, edge and CLV on the site is measured against it. No-vig fair odds →
Fills SIZE · AT THE PRICE · $50 · $200
How much money an exchange's order book takes at the price shown, read from the book itself. A bid counts as filled only when a trade printed through it.
Graded GRADED VS CLOSE · GRADED SIDE · WITH A CLOSE · JUDGED (CLEARS)
A bet with a closing price read, so its CLV is counted. Bets with no close are kept but never judged.
Held-out test
A model scored on games it was never fitted on, against the market's price at the time. It is the only test a model here is judged by.
Hold LOWEST HOLD
The margin left when you take the best price on every side of a market. Under zero means an arb. Hold and arbitrage →
Implied chance CHANCE · PROB · IMPLIED %
The win chance a price stands for: 1 ÷ decimal odds. On an exchange it is the contract price in cents, with the fee added when the screen says fee in.
INFORMATION, NOT AN EDGE
Facts about the market, such as how far a line moved. Useful context; on its own it has graded as no edge.
LEAD LEADS
A price the math likes that the record has not backed: under 50 graded, or graded without passing the test. Treat it as something to check, not a bet.
Lean
The side the fair favours at the app's number, before the payout is taken into account.
Line gap GAP
Two apps hang the same prop at different numbers. A gap alone is not a bet: the payouts can even it out, and the softest number has graded as no edge.
LINE MOVES MOVES · MOVE · CHART
Each venue's price for a side over time, saved every few minutes from when we first saw it. "Since first seen" starts at our first read, not the opening line.
Log loss
A score of how well probabilities matched what happened; lower is better. A model is compared with the market's own log loss on the same games.
Maker MAKER FEE · REST A BID AT · BID · BEST BID
Resting a bid under the ask and waiting for a seller, instead of buying now. It pays a smaller maker fee (none on Kalshi NHL spreads and totals) and fills only if someone sells to you.
Middle MIDDLES
Over at a low number and Under at a higher one, so a result in between wins both bets. It pays only when the result lands in the gap. Middles →
MIN BET SIZE
Shows exchange prices at what that many dollars actually fills at, walking down the order book, and drops prices that cannot take it.
MODEL, NOT AN EDGE
Our own model's number shown next to the market's. In its held-out test the market's price beat the model, so a gap is the model disagreeing, not a bet.
Moved too MOVED AT
Venues whose price followed the biggest move on the same market. "Moved at" is the venue that moved most.
Multiplier PAYS · STD
What a pick'em app pays on a slip, or on one side of a leg (e.g. 0.85x). Std is the app's standard table. Pick’em break-evens →
No-vig NO VIG
A price with the book's margin (the vig) removed, so both sides add up to 100%. Pinnacle's no-vig price is the fair. No-vig fair odds →
One-way
A price posted on one side only, with no other side to take the margin out of, so its fair is read from the main line.
Outright OUTRIGHTS
A bet on who wins an event with many runners (an F1 race, a golf tournament), priced against Pinnacle's no-vig. Pinnacle takes small limits here, so the fair is weaker than on game lines.
Pick'em PICKEM
Apps (PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, DK Pick6) where you pick Over or Under on several player props in one slip, paid from a table. Each leg has to hit more often than the slip's break-even. Pick’em break-evens →
Projection FAR FROM PROJECTION
Our model's expected stat for a player. A line far from it is the model disagreeing with the market, not a bet.
PROVEN (label)
The label the record marks proven only after 50 graded bets of one kind at one venue read direct, CLV above zero with a t-stat of 2 or more against Pinnacle's close, and results not far below what that CLV predicts. It describes the sample so far, not future results. How the record works →
REF PINNACLE
A sharp reference column (Pinnacle, Matchbook). It sets the fair and is never a place we suggest you bet.
RESEARCH, NOT A BET
A screen that measures an idea while its own record builds, with alerts off. Nothing on it is a recommendation until RESULTS says otherwise.
ROI
Profit divided by the money staked. It needs hundreds of bets to settle, which is why CLV is judged first.
Sharp consensus NO SHARP LINE · NO SHARP MARKET
The fair from the sharp sources that post a prop (Pinnacle, Kalshi). No sharp line means none posts it, so the leg is never priced as a play.
STALE STALE AT PINNACLE
A price not re-read in the last few minutes, or one that stood still while the market moved. Check it in the app before you bet.
Steam
Several venues moving the same way within minutes, usually on news or big money.
Still at old number
Venues that have not followed a move yet. That alone has graded as no edge against the close.
t-stat CLV T
How many standard errors the average CLV sits above zero. The record's rule needs 2 or more, so the result is unlikely to be luck. How the record works →
Taker fee TAKE NOW
What an exchange charges when you buy at the ask right away. Kalshi's is 0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price), rounded up. Exchange fees and effective odds →
TRUST
How far the fair and the price can be relied on: fresh, read direct, a main line and a sharp fair score higher. Hover a row for the reasons.
TX TX ✓ · TEXAS · TEXAS VENUES
A venue open to bettors in Texas: pick'em apps and the CFTC exchanges (Kalshi, Polymarket US, Gemini). No sportsbook is legal there.
Units
Profit counted in stakes of one unit each, so records with different bet sizes compare.
Verdict
Where a kind of bet stands on its own record: a lead while it builds, failed when it lost to the close, and what the rule calls proven once it passes the test.
W-L W-L-P · RECORD
Wins, losses and pushes of graded bets. A win rate means something only next to the break-even it needs.
z Z-SCORE
How many standard errors a hit rate sits above its break-even. 2 is the bar; under about 50 legs it means little.
