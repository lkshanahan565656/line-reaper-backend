Cancel any time

Pro renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time, in a few clicks, from Billing in the app (this opens the secure Stripe portal). You do not have to call or email anyone. When you cancel:

  • you will not be charged again;
  • you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for; and
  • your account becomes a free account after that. You can delete it at any time.

First-payment refund

If you are new to Pro and it is not for you, email us within 72 hours of your first Pro payment and we will refund that payment in full and end your Pro access. This applies once per person and per payment method.

Other refunds

Apart from the first-payment refund, payments are not refundable, and we do not give refunds or credits for part of a billing period, for time you did not use, or because of the results of any bet or trade. Picks and alerts win and lose; a losing result is not a reason for a refund. We will always refund:

  • Billing mistakes, such as a duplicate charge or a charge after you cancelled;
  • Long outages: if Pro is unavailable to you for more than 72 hours in a row because of a problem on our side, you can ask for a refund or credit for the days affected; and
  • Accounts we close without cause: the unused part of your current period.

Nothing here takes away any refund right you have under the law where you live.

How to ask

Email the contact address on our legal page from the address on your account, with the date and amount of the charge. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually show up within 5 to 10 business days.

Card disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; it is almost always faster. An account may be paused while a dispute is open.

Price changes

We will tell you at least 30 days before a price change reaches your subscription, so you can cancel first if you want to.
