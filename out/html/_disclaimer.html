Our results and track record

  • Our own record. The record on our welcome screen is the operator's own betting history, tracked on Pikkit and shown in units. It is one bettor's results over a limited period, it is not typical, and it does not mean you will get similar results.
  • Alert records. Alerts are graded at the price shown when the alert went out. You may not get that price: prices move, platforms limit or reject bets, and fees and slippage apply. Your results will differ.
  • Past results do not guarantee future results. Edges shrink, markets change, and models are wrong.

Models, EV and arbitrage

Probabilities, fair odds, expected value (EV), closing-line value, ratings and trader grades are estimates from statistical models and can be wrong. "Arbitrage" figures assume every leg is filled at the shown price; in practice legs can be limited, voided, re-priced or settled differently by each platform, which can turn a sure-looking profit into a loss. Check every price and every market's rules yourself before acting.

Data accuracy

Odds, lines, prices, stats and results come from third parties and public sources. They can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and feeds can stop without warning. We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of any data.

Traders and wallets
