How the record works

Every flag the app makes is logged before the game and graded after it. A kind of bet is only called proven when the record clears a fixed test.

Grading

  • Every flag is saved with its price and time when it is made, before the result is known.
  • It is graded against Pinnacle’s no-vig closing price (CLV) and, once the game is over, the result (win, loss, push, profit at the price).
  • Exchange flags are also re-read two minutes later, so a price that had gone by then is graded at the price you could have got.
  • Losers stay in. Nothing is removed after the fact.

The test for “proven”

  • At least 50 graded bets of one kind, at one venue read straight from that venue (a copied price never counts).
  • Average CLV above zero with a t-statistic of 2 or more, so the result is unlikely to be luck.
  • Results not far below what the CLV says they should be.
  • Kinds are never pooled to reach 50, and alternate lines, stale lines and pick’em props are judged on their own.

Everything else is a lead: a price the math likes that the record has not yet backed. The app labels every ranked row LEAD or PROVEN, and the Results tab shows the count for each kind as it builds.

What it does not mean

Proven means a kind of bet has beaten the close over the sample so far. It does not promise future results, and a proven kind can stop working as venues adjust. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
