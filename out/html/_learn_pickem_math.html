Pick’em break-evens

PrizePicks, Underdog, DK Pick6 and Betr pay a fixed multiple for a slip. The multiple sets the chance each pick must hit for the slip to break even.

The break-even per pick

A 3-pick PrizePicks Power slip pays 6x: it needs all three, so each pick must hit 55.0% of the time (the cube root of 1/6). At 58% per pick the slip’s EV is +17.1%. A pick has to beat its break-even by more than the app takes for any of this to matter.

These are the standard tables. Apps change them and some reprice slips with picks from the same game: the TOOLS calculator reads DK Pick6’s live table and lets you type the multiple the app shows.

Where the pick’s chance comes from

The chance of a pick comes from a sharp price on the same player and line, with the vig taken out (see no-vig fair odds). Pinnacle prices few player props, and an app’s line can be at a different number from the book’s, so many picks have no defensible chance at all; the app says so instead of guessing.

How the app’s own flagged picks have done against their break-evens is in the Results tab, graded leg by leg.
