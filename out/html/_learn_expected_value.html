Expected value (EV)

EV is what a bet returns on average at the fair chance. A positive EV price pays more than the outcome is worth.

The formula

EV per dollar = fair chance × decimal odds − 1. A side that is fair at 50% and priced +110 (decimal 2.10) has EV 50% × 2.10 − 1 = +5.0%: $5.00 on a $100 bet, on average.

What EV is not

EV is a price compared with a fair. It is only as right as the fair, and a positive number on one bet says nothing on its own: a +3% bet still loses close to half the time. Whether a kind of bet actually wins over time is what the record answers, and the app marks a kind of bet proven only after the test on the record page.

Exchange prices on the odds screen already include the venue’s fee, so their EV is after fees. A price read second-hand (marked c) can be minutes old: check it in the app before you bet.
