Middles

A middle is Over a low number at one venue and Under a higher one at another. If the result lands between them, both bets win.

How it pays

Worked example: Over 44.5 at −110 and Under 46.5 at −110, $100 total
Value
Stake on each$50.00 / $50.00
Result 45 or 46 (both win)$90.91
Anything else (one wins)−$4.55
Break-even chance of landing in the middle4.76%
EV if the middle hits 6% of the time$1.18 (+1.18%)

The bet is worth it only when the chance of landing in the window is above the break-even. That chance depends on the sport and the numbers: NFL final margins land on 3 and 7 far more often than on their neighbours, so a middle across those numbers is worth more than one the same width elsewhere. Middles in the app are tracked and graded like every other flag.
